## Why Water Varies From Town to Town

Lubbock's water comes from the Ogallala Aquifer and from Lake Alan Henry and Lake Meredith, and the City of Lubbock's 2025 report shows groundwater supplying 61 percent. Nearby towns use different mixes of wells and purchased water, so hardness and other values change from place to place. A free water test at your tap gives the number that matters.
