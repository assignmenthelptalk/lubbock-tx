## What Types of Drinking Water Systems Can You Install in Lubbock, TX?

Three drinking water systems cover most homes: under sink systems, countertop purification units and alkaline water systems.

<div class="svc-grid">

<div class="svc-card">



![Under Sink Drinking Water Systems Lubbock TX](../../assets/images/card-under-sink-drinking-water-systems-lubbock-tx.webp)



### Under Sink Drinking Water Systems

An under-sink drinking water system installs in the cabinet and delivers filtered water to a dedicated faucet. Options range from carbon filters to full [reverse osmosis](/reverse-osmosis-installation/). It gives the best balance of water quality, flow and convenience for a kitchen. Roughly 60 to 70 percent of Lubbock's city supply is Ogallala groundwater, according to City of Lubbock water quality reports, and many homeowners add an under-sink system to improve taste. Total dissolved solids run around 620 mg/L, which is why many homes pair a softener with an under-sink reverse osmosis system.

</div>

<div class="svc-card">



![Countertop Purification Units Lubbock TX](../../assets/images/card-countertop-purification-units-lubbock-tx.webp)



### Countertop Purification Units

A countertop purification unit sits beside the sink and connects to the faucet or fills from a pitcher-style tank. It needs no plumbing work, so it suits renters and small spaces. The tradeoff is lower flow and a smaller capacity than an under-sink system.

</div>

<div class="svc-card">


![Alkaline Water Systems Lubbock TX](/placeholders/card.svg)

### Alkaline Water Systems

An alkaline water system adds a stage that raises the water's pH after [filtration](/water-filtration-systems/). It changes how the water tastes and measures on a pH test. Claims about health benefits are limited, so we explain what the stage does and does not do before you choose it. Lubbock tap water is slightly alkaline, averaging around pH 8.1.

</div>

</div>

### Bottleless Water Cooler Installation

A bottleless water cooler connects to your water line and filters water on demand, so you stop buying and lifting jugs. It suits offices, clinics and homes that use a lot of drinking water. We install the line, the drain if needed, and the filter set.

> **Not a good fit if...** you want to treat the whole house. A drinking water system treats one tap, so hardness elsewhere remains. A [water softener system](/water-softener-systems/) handles the rest.


## Which drinking water system fits your home or office?

Choose by space, flow and quality target. Under sink gives the best all-round result, countertop is simplest, and a cooler suits busy offices. A free estimate compares them for you.
